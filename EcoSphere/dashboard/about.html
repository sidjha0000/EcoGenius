About EcoGenius

EcoGenius is a platform designed to help individuals make informed decisions about sustainable living and eco-friendly practices. Our goal is to provide users with resources and tools to reduce their carbon footprint, adopt sustainable habits, and contribute to a greener planet.

Our Mission

At EcoGenius, our mission is to make eco-friendly living more accessible and practical for everyone. Through our quiz-based app and various resources like recycling tips, eco-friendly product suggestions, and air quality monitoring, we aim to raise awareness and help users take small steps toward making a big difference.

Key Features

  • Eco Score Quiz: Take our interactive quiz to assess your eco-friendliness and receive tips on improving your score.
  • Eco Map: Discover nearby eco-friendly stores, recycling centers, green zones, and sustainable transport options.
  • Air Quality Index (AQI): Check real-time air quality data in your area and take steps to improve the environment.
  • Educational Resources: Learn about recycling, sustainable living, and green practices through curated articles and tips.

Why Choose EcoGenius?

EcoGenius offers a unique blend of interactive tools, practical resources, and an easy-to-use interface to make sustainable living more achievable for people of all backgrounds. Whether you're just starting to adopt eco-friendly practices or looking to deepen your knowledge, EcoGenius is here to guide and support you on your journey.

Our Vision

We envision a world where sustainable practices are ingrained in everyday life, where eco-friendly choices are easy, affordable, and mainstream. By empowering individuals with the knowledge and tools to make better choices, we can create a collective impact on the planet and future generations.
