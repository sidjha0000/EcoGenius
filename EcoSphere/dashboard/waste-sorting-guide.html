♻️ Waste Sorting Guide

Properly sorting waste helps reduce pollution and conserves resources. Here's a guide to help you sort your waste responsibly.

Recyclable Waste

  • Plastic Bottles
  • Glass Bottles
  • Cardboard Boxes
  • Aluminum Cans
  • Paper (Newspapers, Magazines)

Recyclable materials can be processed and used to make new products. Please rinse containers before recycling them.

Compostable Waste

  • Fruit and Vegetable Scraps
  • Coffee Grounds
  • Tea Bags
  • Grass Clippings
  • Eggshells

These items can be composted to help create nutrient-rich soil for gardening. Avoid composting meat or dairy.

Non-Recyclable Waste

  • Styrofoam
  • Broken Glass
  • Plastic Bags
  • Used Tissues
  • Food-Soiled Paper

Items in this category cannot be recycled and should be disposed of in your regular trash bin.
